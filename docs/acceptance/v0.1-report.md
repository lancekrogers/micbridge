native capture, binding execution, and WAV finalization all passed through the
shipped runtime. Two physical LARK M2 transmitter double presses changed the
session from armed to recording to completed. Independent `file` and `ffprobe`
inspection validated the resulting 351.870-second, 67,559,084-byte RIFF/WAVE as
PCM signed 16-bit little endian, stereo, 48 kHz. The private audio was deleted
after validation and was not committed.
